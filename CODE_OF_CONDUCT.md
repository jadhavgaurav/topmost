# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps build a good community:

- Being kind and patient, and assuming good faith.
- Giving and accepting feedback on the work, not the person.
- Saying plainly what you need, and asking when you do not understand.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after the maintainers have decided, or flooding threads.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow them, and may ban a contributor for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

Report a problem privately to `gaurav.vjadhav01@gmail.com`. Reports are handled in confidence, and the person who reports is protected from retaliation.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, such as issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone represents the project in public.
